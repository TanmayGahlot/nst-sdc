About the club

A place to build, contribute, and learn in public

NST-SDC is the student developer community at Newton School of Technology, Pune. We're a group of builders who'd rather ship something small and real than talk about it — open-source contributions, side projects, and the occasional 2am debugging session together.

Our goal is simple: give every student a place to collaborate, learn, and grow in tech — preparing them for real-world challenges through hands-on projects, mentorship, and a community that pushes each other forward.
