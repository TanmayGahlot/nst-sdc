How to get involved

Two ways in

As a member

  1. Join the club and stay updated through our community channels.
  2. Collaborate on ongoing projects or propose new ideas.
  3. Learn and grow through workshops, events, and mentorship.

As a contributor

  1. Explore our repositories and open issues.
  2. Fork a repo and make meaningful contributions.
  3. Open a pull request and collaborate with the team.
